Resume Summary:

The summary should briefly describe the candidate's background,
main technical skills, experience, and career direction.

Technical Skills:

Technical skills should be grouped clearly and should contain
technologies that the candidate actually knows.

Experience:

Experience descriptions should focus on achievements and measurable
results instead of only listing responsibilities.

Projects:

Projects should mention the technologies used and explain
what problem was solved.

Resume Improvements:

Use clear section headings, concise bullet points,
measurable achievements, relevant technical skills,
and job-specific keywords.

Avoid:

Avoid unnecessary personal information, long paragraphs,
irrelevant skills, spelling mistakes, and unsupported claims.
